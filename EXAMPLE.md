# CreativeX live test and account brief

| Part of the test | What actually happened |
|---|---|
| Search | UK creative production manager vacancies from the past month. |
| Listings returned | **10 vacancies.** |
| Descriptions analysed | **5.** Five other records lacked a usable company domain. |
| Qualification | Three analysed records were rejected; one intermediary listing was held because the actual employer identity was unclear. |
| Complete result | **The Perfume Shop** passed through hiring research, advertising checks, contact lookup and brief creation. |
| Advertising | The successful lookup contained **20 unique active ad records matching the company**. This is a sample, not its total ad count. |
| People | **14 employee records returned; three relevant contact candidates selected.** |
| Execution | Completed after fixes and resuming the same batch. All **41 automated tests passed**. |

Here’s the actual account brief in the form a rep could receive:

| Field | The Perfume Shop |
|---|---|
| **Hiring signal** | **Design Operations & Production Manager**, High Wycombe, posted **9 September 2026**. [Job listing](https://www.jobs24.co.uk/job/design-operations-production-manager-127373854) |
| **What caught our attention** | “Oversee the end-to-end creative production process, ensuring work is accurately briefed, tracked, quality checked and delivered to a high standard.” |
| **Why the scope matters** | The role coordinates production across **online, social, CRM, print, campaign and in-store channels**. |
| **Advertising evidence** | **20 matching active Meta ad records** in the sample. [Example 1](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1313614647516212) · [Example 2](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=37437974175847749) · [Example 3](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1057420673422126) |
| **Why CreativeX could be relevant** | The company explicitly needs coordinated creative production and quality checks. That creates a sensible conversation about checking digital assets against brand and channel requirements and reporting consistently across the team. |
| **Suggested first person** | **Jona McKnight, Senior Creative and Production Manager.** The closest functional match among the returned candidates. |
| **Conversation starter** | “Saw you’re hiring a Design Operations & Production Manager to coordinate creative across online, social, CRM and in-store. How does the team check brand and channel requirements across the digital assets?” |
| **Before outreach** | Check CreativeX’s account ownership/customer records and confirm the person’s remit. The vacancy doesn’t establish missing software or buying intent. |
